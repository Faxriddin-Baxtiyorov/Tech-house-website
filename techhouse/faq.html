Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our products, services, and policies.

Delivery & Shipping

How long does delivery take?

Standard delivery takes 2-3 business days for orders over £50 (free) or £4.99 for smaller orders. Express next-day delivery is available for £9.99. Gold members receive free delivery on all orders.

Can I collect my order from a store?

Yes! You can choose in-store collection at checkout. Items are typically ready for pickup within 2 hours of order confirmation. Collection is free of charge at any of our 5 store locations.

Do you deliver internationally?

Currently, we deliver within the United Kingdom only. We're working on expanding our delivery network to other countries in the future.

Returns & Warranty

What is your return policy?

We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee. Items must be in original packaging and unused condition. Bronze members get 30-day returns, Silver members get 45 days, and Gold members get 60 days.

What warranty do products come with?

All products come with a comprehensive 2-year warranty covering manufacturing defects and faults. Gold and Platinum members can extend their warranty for free or at discounted rates.

How do I return an item?

You can initiate a return through your account profile, contact our customer service team, or visit any of our stores. We'll provide a prepaid return label for your convenience.

Products & Services

Do you offer installation services?

Yes! We offer professional installation services for large appliances. Silver members receive free installation, while standard installation fees apply for other customers. Contact us for a quote.

Are your products energy efficient?

Absolutely! We prioritize energy-efficient products. Most of our appliances have A+++ energy ratings, helping you save on utility bills while reducing your carbon footprint.

Do you have smart home compatible products?

Yes! We have a wide range of smart home appliances that can be controlled via smartphone apps and voice assistants. Check our Smart Home category for all compatible products.

Membership & Rewards

How do I join the rewards program?

You can join our Bronze membership for free by creating an account. Silver (£4.99/mo) and Gold (£9.99/mo) memberships offer additional benefits. Visit our Rewards page to learn more.

How do loyalty points work?

You earn points on every purchase: Bronze members earn 1 point per £1, Silver members earn 1.5 points per £1, and Gold members earn 2 points per £1. Points can be redeemed for discounts on future purchases.

Can I cancel my membership?

Yes, you can cancel your Silver or Gold membership at any time through your account settings. You'll retain access until the end of your current billing period.

Payment & Orders

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), PayPal, Apple Pay, and Google Pay. Business customers can arrange invoice payments.

Can I track my order?

Yes! Once your order is confirmed, you'll receive a tracking number via email. You can track your order status in your account profile or by contacting customer service.

What if my product arrives damaged?

If your product arrives damaged, please contact us immediately within 48 hours. We'll arrange a replacement or full refund at no cost to you. Take photos of the damage for our records.

Still have questions?

Our customer service team is here to help 24/7
