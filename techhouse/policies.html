Return & Refund Policy

Return Period

You have 30 days from the date of delivery to return items. Bronze members get 30 days, Silver members get 45 days, and Gold members get 60 days.

Return Conditions

Items must be in original packaging, unused, and in the same condition as received. Include all accessories, manuals, and original tags.

Refund Process

Once we receive and inspect your return, we'll process your refund within 5-10 business days. Refunds will be issued to the original payment method.

Non-Returnable Items

Customized products, gift cards, and items damaged by misuse are not eligible for return. Software and digital products may have different return policies.

Warranty Policy

Standard Warranty

All products come with a 2-year warranty covering manufacturing defects and faults under normal use conditions.

Warranty Coverage

The warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship. It does not cover damage from accidents, misuse, normal wear and tear, or unauthorized repairs.

Warranty Claims

To make a warranty claim, contact our customer service with your order number and a description of the issue. We may require photos or return of the product for inspection.

Extended Warranty

Gold and Platinum members receive free warranty extensions. Extended warranties are also available for purchase at checkout.

Delivery Policy

Delivery Areas

We currently deliver within the United Kingdom only. Delivery times may vary by location.

Delivery Charges

Standard delivery: Free for orders over £50, £4.99 otherwise. Express delivery: £9.99. In-store collection: Free.

If you experience any issues with delivery, contact us immediately. We'll investigate and work to resolve the issue promptly.
