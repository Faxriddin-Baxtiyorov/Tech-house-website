Policies & Information

Delivery & Pickup

Home Delivery: Standard delivery (2-3 business days) - Free for orders over £50, £4.99 otherwise. Express delivery (next day) - £9.99. Gold members receive free delivery on all orders.

In-Store Collection: Available at all store locations. Items ready for pickup within 2 hours of order confirmation. Free of charge.

Warranty & Returns

Product Warranty: All products come with a comprehensive 2-year warranty covering manufacturing defects and faults. Extended warranties available for Gold and Platinum members.

Returns Policy: 30-day money-back guarantee. Items must be in original packaging and unused condition. Bronze members get extended 30-day returns, while higher tier members receive 45-60 day return windows.

After-Sales Support

Our dedicated after-sales support team provides technical assistance, installation guidance, and maintenance services. Professional installation available for large appliances. Annual maintenance packages available for Platinum members.
